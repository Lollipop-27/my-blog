# Project 1 — Weekly Weblog: Barbican Conservatory

## Week 2

### Monday, September 28

Today, we showed our individual prototypes to the rest of the group. Each of us had developed different ideas based on our experiences from the previous week, so this gave us a chance to compare what we had come up with and discuss what could actually work for the final experience.

We talked through the different elements we wanted to keep and how we could combine them into one final approach. At this point, we were moving away from just collecting observations and starting to think more practically about how we could turn our research into something that other people could experience.

### Tuesday, September 29

Today, we started building the actual experience. We worked on putting together the physical elements and figuring out how the different parts would connect.

We also had a discussion with Tonisha about our idea. She suggested that we think about involving the other students in the classroom rather than creating an experience that only one person could interact with at a time. This made us reconsider how we wanted the audience to participate.

From this discussion, we came up with the idea of adding a broadcasting element. Instead of having everything happen only for the person directly interacting with the experience, we could share part of it with the rest of the class. We thought this would make the experience more collective and allow everyone to be involved in some way.

We also made small samples of some of our materials for the other students to touch. This gave us a simple way to test the textures and see how people physically reacted to them.

### Wednesday, September 30

Today, we focused more on testing the technology. We went back through the setup and tried out the different technical elements to make sure everything worked together.

After testing, we made the final adjustments and finished the physical product. It was useful to test everything before the presentation because it helped us identify some of the things that could go wrong and gave us a chance to make changes.

### Thursday, October 1

Today, we presented our final experience to the class. Just before presenting, we had some technical problems. OBS was not connecting properly, so we had to switch to using FaceTime instead. The audio also did not work as well as we expected, so we ended up playing the sound directly through our phones.

Although these problems were frustrating, we were still able to adapt and present the experience. It reminded me that when working with technology, having an alternative method is important because things may not always work as planned.

### Friday, October 2

Today, we started the next project, **Latency**, with a new group of teammates. We had a 30-minute meeting where we shared our own experiences of latency in the digital world, such as delays and interruptions when using technology.

The meeting was mainly about making sure everyone understood the brief and giving us a starting point for developing ideas together. Since this was a new group, it was also useful to hear how other people interpreted the topic and what kinds of experiences they connected with latency.
